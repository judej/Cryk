## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] P05-1. Schedule two briefings in the configured zone. Cover date-only deadlines, holidays, repeated local times, skipped times, and one current catch-up briefing.

- [ ] P05-3. Show known due items during provider outages. Reserve capacity for due work. Distinguish due-item latency from source and analysis lag.
- [ ] P05-4. Recheck audience and ownership for previews and notifications. Use no-store previews and suppress remote resources.
